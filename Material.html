Horor In The Loose

Most wanted

Residents of Millbrook woke on Saturday to find their pumpkin patches smashed and their fences clawed open. Sheriff Harlan Reed confirms that three farmers have reported the same sight: a tall figure with a glowing, grinning head,
walking between the fields after midnight. Witnesses describe a hollow, rattling laugh that carries across the valley. Police have named the suspect only as “Jack,” and he is considered extremely dangerous. Anyone who recognizes
the face pictured is urged to contact the sheriff’s office at once, and to keep their distance.

Old-timers say the creature first appeared in 1893, the night the last harvest was cancelled. Since then, lanterns have been left burning in every window to keep him away. Tonight, the town council has ordered all porch lights lit until dawn.
Children are to be indoors by sunset, and no one should answer a knock after dark. If you hear laughter in the corn, do not investigate. Lock your door and wait for morning.
